AR / VR (Spatial)

Builds immersive experiences for headsets, phones, and the metaverse.

What you actually do

You create augmented and virtual reality apps: training sims, games, virtual meetings, and 3D product viewers.

A day might include

  • Build 3D scenes and interactions
  • Optimize for headsets
  • Integrate hand/eye tracking
  • Profile performance

Skills you need

  • Unity / Unreal
  • 3D math
  • C# / C++
  • WebXR
  • UX for immersion

Tools & tags

Unity3DWebXR

Is it a safe job after 3 years?

Medium job security

Promising but early; adoption swings with hardware. Skills overlap with game dev and 3D web, which gives fallback options.

The future

Apple Vision Pro and mixed reality could expand the market; still maturing.

Outlook: Emerging. Exciting, but treat as a bet rather than a sure thing yet.
