UX / UI Design

Designs how products look, feel, and are used — from research to pixels.

What you actually do

You research users, sketch flows, and craft interfaces that are both beautiful and usable. Bridges business goals and human needs.

A day might include

  • Interview and observe users
  • Wireframe and prototype in Figma
  • Run usability tests
  • Hand off specs to developers

Skills you need

  • Figma / design tools
  • User research
  • Visual design
  • Prototyping
  • Design systems

Is it a safe job after 3 years?

Medium job security

Design is valuable but can be competitive and affected by hiring freezes. AI tools speed up production, so focus on research and strategy, which are hard to automate.

The future

AI generates mockups fast; the differentiator becomes understanding users and business, not just drawing screens.

Outlook: Good but cyclical. Pair design with research or frontend to widen options.

Where to learn

  • Google UX Design (Coursera)
  • NN/g articles
  • Figma learn
  • Refactoring UI (book)
