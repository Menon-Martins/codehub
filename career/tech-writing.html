Tech Writing

Writes docs, tutorials, and manuals that teach people to use and build software.

What you actually do

You turn complex technical topics into clear docs, API references, and guides. Great for people who love language and tech.

A day might include

  • Write tutorials and API docs
  • Test instructions yourself
  • Work with engineers
  • Maintain style guides

Skills you need

  • Clear writing
  • Technical curiosity
  • Markdown/HTML
  • Diagrams
  • Empathy for readers

Is it a safe job after 3 years?

Medium job security

Often a smaller team and early to be cut in freezes, but developer-focused docs are valued by product companies. AI drafts; humans edit and structure.

The future

AI generates first drafts; the editor who ensures accuracy and clarity becomes the value.

Outlook: Steady but niche. Combine with dev skills to raise demand.
