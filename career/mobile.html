Mobile

Builds native and cross-platform apps for phones and tablets (iOS, Android).

What you actually do

You build the apps people carry in their pocket: chats, banking, delivery, games. Can be native (Swift/Kotlin) or cross-platform (Flutter, React Native).

A day might include

  • Implement screens and navigation
  • Integrate with device features (camera, GPS)
  • Publish to App Store / Play Store
  • Fix crashes and battery issues

Skills you need

  • Swift or Kotlin
  • A cross-platform tool (Flutter/React Native)
  • Mobile UI patterns
  • App store publishing
  • Offline & performance tuning

Tools & tags

KotlinSwiftFlutter

Is it a safe job after 3 years?

Medium-High job security

Mobile is huge (billions of devices) but native demand can shift with platform trends. Cross-platform skills keep you adaptable; companies still need people to ship and maintain apps.

The future

Cross-platform frameworks reduce the need for two separate native teams, so learn one native + one cross-platform stack.

Outlook: Solid. Apps aren't going away; maintenance and new features keep teams busy.
