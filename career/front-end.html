Front-end

Builds the visual interface and experience of websites and apps — what the user sees and touches.

What you actually do

You turn designs into real, interactive screens: buttons that respond, forms that validate, animations that feel smooth. You work closely with design and back-end to make products usable on phones, tablets, and desktops.

A day might include

  • Build UI components with React/Vue/Svelte
  • Fix layout and accessibility bugs
  • Connect screens to APIs
  • Improve page speed and responsiveness

Skills you need

  • HTML & semantic markup
  • CSS (Flexbox/Grid, responsive)
  • JavaScript (ES2023+)
  • A framework (React, Vue or Svelte)
  • Basic accessibility (a11y)

Is it a safe job after 3 years?

High job security

Every product needs a face. Front-end demand stays strong, especially for engineers who also understand performance and accessibility. AI helps generate UI but still needs humans to ship, test, and maintain it.

The future

More component libraries, design systems, and AI-assisted building — but the need to craft fast, accessible experiences keeps growing.

Outlook: Stable to growing. Companies always need someone to own the screen users touch.
