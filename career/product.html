Product / Management

Decides what gets built and connects business, tech, and users.

What you actually do

You set the vision and priorities: talk to users, define features, and keep the team aligned. Less coding, more decisions and communication.

A day might include

  • Talk to users and stakeholders
  • Write specs and roadmaps
  • Prioritize the backlog
  • Measure outcomes

Skills you need

  • Communication
  • Agile/Scrum
  • Data literacy
  • Empathy
  • Decision-making

Is it a safe job after 3 years?

Medium job security

High impact and pay, but roles are fewer and more exposed in downturns. Strong PMs who understand tech deeply are valued.

The future

AI helps with docs and analysis; human judgment on trade-offs stays central.

Outlook: Rewarding but competitive. Understanding engineering makes you safer.
